---
title: "Specimen general record"
sidebar:
  order: 0
---

The general record stores the specimen’s working identifier, responsible people, taxon, preparation context, and condition. Depending on project settings, the Field ID uses a Cataloger’s initials and field number or a project prefix, catalog number, and suffix.

Choose the taxon from the project registry; a taxonomic name is an identification, while the specimen record represents the documented `dwc:Occurrence` and may have a `dwc:basisOfRecord` such as `PreservedSpecimen`.

Record Cataloger, Preparator, and Determiner according to their actual work. Describe condition at preparation from direct evidence and preserve collection and preparation times when known.
