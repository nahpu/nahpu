---
title: "Specimen taxonomy"
sidebar:
  order: 0
---

Taxonomic fields are populated from the taxon selected in the specimen record. They describe the taxonomic identification, not the physical specimen itself.

Class, order, family, genus, and specific epithet follow the registered taxon. Edit the taxon registry when the shared name record is wrong; change the specimen’s selected taxon when only that identification is wrong. Preserve scientific-name authorship and identification notes when available, and record the responsible Determiner rather than inferring one.
