Environmental data records the weather and other conditions for this event. To open it, select the cloud icon in the event's tab panel, which is the last panel on a phone and the bottom-right panel on a desktop app. In `Settings` > `Events` > `Environmental data fields`, choose which built-in fields to display. Hiding a field does not delete its stored values or exclude them from exports.

Record measurements in the displayed units: temperature in °C, humidity in percent, and rainfall in millimeters. Cloud cover uses codes 0–8 for eighths of the sky covered, or 9 when the sky cannot be observed during the event (e.g., the event is inside a cave or building). Leave unmeasured values empty; NAHPU treats zero as a measurement.

A duplicated event starts with blank environmental data.
