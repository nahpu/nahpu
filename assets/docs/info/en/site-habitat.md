Record the habitat observed at the site, not a broad habitat inferred only from a map. Use controlled habitat type and condition values consistently across the project.

In the description, note evidence that helps interpret the collecting environment, such as dominant vegetation, substrate, canopy or ground cover, water conditions, disturbance, and land use. Keep methods, sampling equipment, and effort in the collecting event instead.

## Darwin Core context

Habitat type, condition, and description are combined into a single `dwc:habitat` value, separated with " | ". Canopy cover is exported as a measurement of the collecting event rather than as a habitat term.
