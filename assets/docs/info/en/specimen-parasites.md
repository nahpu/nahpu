A parasite record documents parasite material or an observed organism interaction associated with the host specimen.

Keep the host association and parasite identifiers consistent with the sample labels. A blank parasite section does not establish that parasites were absent. Use the parasite examined and detected fields in the specimen attributes when that distinction matters.
