Choose the primary activity that best describes the collecting event. Record secondary activities, unusual circumstances, or work represented by `Other` in the notes.

Activity is a project-level categorization for consistent filtering and reporting. The actual sampling procedure belongs in effort records and may be exported as `dwc:samplingProtocol`. Do not use the activity label as a substitute for equipment, duration, quantity, or other sampling-effort details.
