---
title: "Specimen capture"
sidebar:
  order: 0
---

Capture information connects the specimen to the collecting event that provides its site, sampling context, date range, and participating personnel. Record the specimen-specific capture date, time, method, collector, and coordinate choice when they differ from or refine the event.

Changing the collecting event changes the record’s sampling context. NAHPU clears dependent capture fields except date and time so values from the previous event are not silently retained. Review every field after changing the event and never infer a coordinate when the available evidence does not identify one.
