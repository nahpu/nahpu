---
title: "Specimen parts"
sidebar:
  order: 0
---

Specimen parts document physical material derived from or associated with the specimen, such as a skin, skull, skeleton, tissue, organ, slide, or other preparation. Each part should have the identifiers needed to match its label or container.

Record material type, preparation or treatment, barcode or tissue ID, responsible Preparator, and remarks. In structured exports a part can be represented as a Darwin Core `dwc:MaterialEntity`; preparation information maps to `dwc:preparations`. Configure controlled part types and treatments in Settings and use them consistently.
