Attributes include the measurements and biological observations made for the specimen, such as sex, life stage, reproductive condition, measurements, and other fields specific to the taxonomic group of focus.

Enter only observed or documented values and keep the displayed unit. Use `Unknown` or leave a field empty according to the project protocol; do not use zero for an unmeasured value.

For fishes, first choose `Individual` for one fish or `Lot` for many individuals stored together; a lot records total individuals, length and weight ranges, and the individuals measured.

Use custom fields for additional observations not captured by the built-in specimen attribute fields. Check their type, unit convention, and catalog applicability before entering values.
