---
title: "Specimen attributes"
sidebar:
  order: 0
---

Attributes contain measurements and biological observations made for the specimen, including sex, life stage or age, reproductive condition, morphometrics, and taxon-specific fields.

Enter only observed or documented values and retain the displayed unit. Use `Unknown` or leave a field empty according to the project protocol rather than guessing. Measurements can become Darwin Core `MeasurementOrFact` data, while sex, life stage, and reproductive condition can map to their corresponding Darwin Core terms. Notes should explain qualifiers, damage, uncertainty, or a method that affects interpretation.
