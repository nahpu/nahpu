When creating an event, under `Event Personnel`, `Add` every project member who took part in or supported the collecting event, and assign the project-defined event role (e.g., Leader or Helper) that describes their work. This can include field collectors, trap-line personnel, guides, drivers, and other contributors.

The options for `Event Personnel` come from the project personnel. A person must be added to the project before they can be added to an event. We recommend reviewing the participant list after duplicating an event.

Event Personnel roles are distinct from the NAHPU specimen-care roles `Cataloger`, `Preparator`, and `Determiner`. Event Personnel roles document who was present and working at a collecting event, and in what capacity, not who prepared or identified the specimens.
