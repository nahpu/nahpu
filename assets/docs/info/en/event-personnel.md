`Add` everyone who took part in or supported the collecting event, and assign the project-defined event role that describes their work. This can include field collectors, trap-line personnel, guides, drivers, and other contributors.

Event participation is distinct from the NAHPU specimen-care roles `Cataloger`, `Preparator`, and `Determiner`. A person listed here documents who was present and working at an event, not who prepared or identified the specimens.

Review the participant list after duplicating an event. `Add` a missing person to the project personnel before assigning them here.
