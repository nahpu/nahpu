Choose a backup, transfer, structured export, or publishing bundle according to the next use.

## Steps

1. For disaster recovery, choose `Backup database`.
2. For combining NAHPU devices, choose `Export project`; use JSON.GZ without media or ZIP/TAR.GZ with media.
3. For spreadsheets or analysis, choose `Export records`.
4. For standards-based sharing, choose `Bundle records` and select Darwin Core Archive, Darwin Core Data Package, or NAHPU Data Package.
5. For labels or reports, choose `Export documents`.
