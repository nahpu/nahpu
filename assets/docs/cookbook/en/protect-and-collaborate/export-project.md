Create a project transfer that another NAHPU installation can merge.

## Steps

1. Open the source project drawer and select `Export project`.
2. Review record counts and warnings for missing media.
3. Choose light `JSON.GZ` only for records without media, or ZIP/TAR.GZ for a complete transfer with available media.
4. Export and store or send the archive securely.
5. Retain the source data until the receiving device has completed and verified its merge.
