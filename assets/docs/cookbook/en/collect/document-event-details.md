Describe how sampling was conducted, by whom, and under which conditions.

## Steps

1. Choose the primary activity and add notes for secondary or unusual work.
2. Add each sampling method, equipment count or size, and method notes as effort records.
3. Add everyone who participated and assign the appropriate event role.
4. Record weather measurements with their units and observation context.
5. Review automatically derived astronomy values and correct the event time or location if they are unexpected.
