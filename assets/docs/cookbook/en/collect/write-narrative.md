Record field context that does not belong in a structured site, event, or specimen field.

## Steps

1. Open Narratives and select `+`.
2. Enter the date, time, writer, and related Site ID.
3. Write concise Markdown-supported observations, decisions, and context.
4. Attach relevant media when it adds evidence or context.
5. Preview the entry and save it.
